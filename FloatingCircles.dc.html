Floating Circles

Tehran, Iran
Summer 2020
Cooperation with Dahi Studio
One month
Role: Ideation, Fabrication, Drawings Documents

In the practice of architecture, it is often necessary to push disciplinary boundaries to solve more complex problems. In this vein, parametric design gets nurtured from creative abilities such as critical thinking, problem solving and even the designers’ intuition in order to integrate a higher number of variable approaches to solve problems through parallel or recursive schemes. Accordingly, the Floating Circles is the story of design and fabrication of a parametric ceiling for the interior design of Mofid brokerage.

Rhino | Grasshopper | C# | V-ray

Images are taken by Yasaman Ashjazadeh

Design Process

PROBLEM
The place which was considered to be one of the new branches of Mofid brokerage had a height of 6 meters which was not appropriate and needed a ceiling to attract people’s attention.
SOLUTION
As the first step of design, a surface with a curvature based on different zoning in the place was generated. The private area with the smallest height, the entrance and the public area with the larger height were designed. Moreover, the whole surface was suspended at the appropriate height from the ceiling.
